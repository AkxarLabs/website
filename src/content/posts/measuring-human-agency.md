Human-centered AI research needs measurements for agency, not only task speed or model accuracy.

## Working Definition

Agency means that people can understand the system, direct it toward their goals, recover from mistakes, and decide when not to use it.

## Design Implications

Interfaces should make uncertainty visible, keep user intent editable, and preserve a meaningful audit trail. Research artifacts can include user studies, interface prototypes, and policy recommendations.
