AI systems shape institutions through repeated small interactions. Simulation can help teams inspect those effects before release.

## Approach

The basic pattern is to model stakeholders, incentives, information flows, and interventions. The goal is not to perfectly predict society; it is to compare plausible failure modes and make assumptions visible.

## Example Questions

- Which users benefit from a model behavior, and which users bear the cost?
- Does the system amplify existing bottlenecks?
- What monitoring would reveal harm early enough to change course?
